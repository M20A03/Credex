# GTM — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target User
**Primary:** Solo technical founders and CTOs at Indian SaaS/AI startups with 2-20 employees spending ₹5,000–₹2,00,000/month on AI tools.
**Secondary:** Engineering managers at 20-100 person companies who control AI tooling budget.
**Psychographic:** Cost-conscious but quality-focused. Values INR pricing transparency. Active on IndiaHacks, PEPilani alumni networks, StartupIndia forums.

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)

### Week 1: Seeding (Target: 25 users)
- Post audit results screenshot on @credex Twitter with "My team was wasting ₹48,000/year on AI tools. Here's what I found →"
- Submit to IndiaHacks newsletter (free startup product feature)
- Share in WhatsApp groups: "YC India Founders", "B2B SaaS India", "Bangalore Startup Network"
- Direct message 10 founder friends personally, ask for honest feedback

### Week 2: Community Distribution (Target: 40 more users)
- Post on Hacker News "Show HN": "Show HN: Free AI tool spend auditor for Indian startups (₹ INR pricing)"
- ProductHunt launch (free, high-traffic for tools)
- Write tweet thread: "6 AI tools, ₹1.8L/year. How we cut it to ₹90K without losing productivity"
- Answer relevant questions on Reddit r/IndiaTech, r/IndianStartups

### Week 3: Leverage Virality (Target: 35 more users)
- Activate share button on results — every shared audit is a free ad
- Reach out to 5 Indian founder newsletters (The Morning Context, Signal newsletter, SaaSBOOMi)
- Offer free audit analysis call to first 20 users (learn + convert)

### Week 4: Retention Loop (Target: 25+ returning users)
- Email follow-up to all leads: "Your AI tools got more expensive this month — re-audit free"
- Launch referral: "Share your audit, get a detailed Credex consultation call for free"

## Why This Works
The tool is inherently shareable — results with "I saved ₹2.4L/year" are social currency in founder communities. The ₹ INR framing makes it uniquely relevant to Indian founders tired of USD-centric SaaS tools.
